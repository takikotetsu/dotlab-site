What .Lab can promise

● Pull the card — nothing breaks

If a card gets yanked or a reader freezes mid-copy, .Lab notices within seconds and stops safely. Plug it back in and it resumes, skipping everything already verified. Insert a similar-looking wrong card and it refuses. Built by literally ripping cards out, over and over.

● The free tier verifies like Pro

Everything that keeps your data safe works identically in the free tier — no compromises. Pro adds multi-destination backups, automation and reports. Safety is never behind a paywall. That’s policy.

● “Probably copied” isn’t in the vocabulary

.Lab reads written files back and compares them, then reads the source card once more to double-check — only then does it say “done.” Paranoia is the default setting, catching even one-off glitches from readers and cables.

● Built on set, updated at set speed

Developed hand-in-hand with working camera crews — field feedback often ships as an update the same day. English and Japanese UI included.

Features

● Your originals are untouchable

Source media is opened read-only, by design. Overwriting or deleting originals is structurally impossible. Until your backup is verified, your originals stay exactly as they were.

● Power loss? Resume where you left off

Every step is journaled as it happens. After a crash or power cut, .Lab picks up where it stopped, skipping verified files. Half-written files never masquerade as finished ones.

● Two backups in one pass

Read the card once, write to multiple SSDs simultaneously (Pro). Chain copies for backups-of-backups, plus a watch-folder mode that copies new files as they appear.

● Proof, not promises

Export verification results as a thumbnail PDF report or industry-standard ASC MHL (Pro). Hand clients evidence — and answer “did it really copy?” with receipts.

● Insert card, ready to go

Auto-detects Sony, Canon, RED, ARRI and Blackmagic cards as well as regular SD cards (DCIM). Insert a card and it’s set as the source, reel name suggested automatically.

● Check your footage on the spot

Double-click a finished reel to browse thumbnails, play clips and inspect metadata. Get completion alerts by chime, macOS notification — or email when you’re away from the cart.

FAQ

Does it really never touch my originals?
Yes. Source media is opened read-only in exactly one place in the code, and no write or delete path exists. It’s a design principle, continuously enforced by the test suite.
What if power dies mid-copy?
All state is committed to an SQLite journal as it happens. On relaunch, .Lab detects the interrupted job and resumes, skipping verified files. Partially written files never end up under their final name (atomic commits).
Which cameras and formats are supported?
Copying and verification are format-agnostic — every file type works. Card auto-detection and metadata cover Sony XAVC/X-OCN, Canon, RED, ARRI, Blackmagic RAW and still-photo cards.
Can I use it offline on location?
Yes. Copying and verification are fully local, and your license has a 30-day offline grace period.
